# Design discussion

Base the design on the requested outcome, applicable constraints, and accepted choices.
Resolve prerequisite decisions first and delegated details directly.
Carry accepted choices forward unless the basis for the choice has changed.
During an ongoing design discussion, connect the current point to the overall design.
When another unresolved decision is needed for the requested design and its prerequisites are settled, address it in the same response with a concrete recommendation, its effects and material tradeoffs.
Ask for a user decision when the design depends on a choice that requires the user's agreement and has not been delegated.
Record unresolved matters for the requested Design in its consolidated list, identifying the decisions or work they affect.
Fill gaps in project facts and prior decision reasons from relevant contracts, code, and [Architecture Memory](../../architecture-memory/SKILL.md).

For multi-topic discussions, use [progress navigation](design-navigation.md).
