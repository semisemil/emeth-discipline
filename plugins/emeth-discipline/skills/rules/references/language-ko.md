## Korean writing

When writing Korean, write for a reader who holds less context than you and tires when meaning must be inferred.

- Choose the word whose meaning the context fixes at once.
  When a short native Korean word has several meanings or a broad sense, use the Sino-Korean word, technical term or loanword readers already use for that concept: 이유 over 까닭, 집계 over 세기, 비율 or 할당 over 몫, 방향 over 쪽, 속도 over 빠르기, 경로 or 방법 over 길.
- Name a previously introduced concept again when it is a sentence or more away or when several candidates fit.
  Write "검증 단계" rather than "그 스텝", and "`status` 열에 들어간다" rather than "이 칸에 선다".
