# Architecture Co-op

Apply [../rules/references/design-discussion.md](../rules/references/design-discussion.md) when working through architecture decisions with the user.
Use [ensemble](../ensemble/SKILL.md) with [../../references/design-exploration.md](../../references/design-exploration.md) for its candidate assignments and synthesis.
Connect each choice to the overall structure and behavior, revising affected choices when gaps or conflicts emerge.
Use [../development-design/references/document-form.md](../development-design/references/document-form.md) to synthesize and present the resulting architecture.
Complete when important in-scope issues are resolved and the structure and behavior form a coherent whole; present the resulting architecture and its completion status.
Then record the agreed decisions and structure under [Architecture Memory](../architecture-memory/SKILL.md).
